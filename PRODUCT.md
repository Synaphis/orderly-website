# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

iPhone shoppers who buy across many stores and need a calmer way to remember what happened after checkout: deliveries, returns, refunds, receipts, warranty dates, and support proof.

## Product Purpose

Orderly turns screenshots, receipts, shipping updates, return labels, and order pages into organized purchase records. The website should explain the app clearly for users and App Store review without pretending to show app screens.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, practical.

## Anti-references

No device frames, fake app UI, generated screenshots, busy sticker scenes, loud SaaS color fields, pre-release signup language, or vague illustrations that do not explain the after-buying workflow.

## Design Principles

- Show the after-buying mess in simple concrete terms.
- Use restrained illustrations that clarify receipts, returns, refunds, warranties, and saved proof.
- Keep the site minimal and readable so policy pages feel trustworthy.
- Make privacy and support easy to find.
- Treat Orderly as an available product, not a coming-soon concept.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, 44px minimum interactive targets, semantic navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion-safe interactions, and responsive layouts that avoid text overlap.
